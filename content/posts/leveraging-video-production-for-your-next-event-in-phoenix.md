## Introduction

Phoenix has something happening all year round, from music festivals to conferences and everything in between. With that much going on, it's hard to make your event the one people remember. Video helps. Here's what it can do for your next event in Phoenix:

1. **Capture the whole thing**: Keynotes, panels, networking and the after party. Attendees get to relive it, and people who missed it get to see what they missed.
2. **Promote it**: Before the event, video builds excitement. After, highlights and attendee stories sell the next one.
3. **Keep people engaged**: Live streams and short clips during the event keep your audience involved, whether they're in the room or not.
4. **Give you footage that keeps working**: One shoot can feed your social media, emails and website long after the doors close.
5. **Show off your people**: Video puts your organizers, sponsors and speakers front and center, which builds trust and opens doors to future partnerships.

Here are three ways to put that to work.

## Highlight Reel

A highlight reel is a short film of the best moments of your event. It's how people who couldn't make it get a taste, and how people who were there get to feel it again. It's also your best tool for selling next year. We've made aftermovies for Relentless Beats festivals like Decadence Arizona, which takes over Phoenix Raceway for two nights, so fans can relive it and everyone who missed it sees what they're in for next time.

## Live Streaming

Streaming your event lets people join from anywhere. It's especially useful for conferences and keynote speakers, where the ideas are worth sharing beyond the room. It also gives attendees something easy to share with their friends while the event is still happening. Record the stream too, and your best sessions can be watched on demand for weeks.

## Testimonials

Nothing sells an event like the people who went. Short interviews with attendees give future guests real reasons to come, in real voices. Use them on your website, on social media and in your emails. Keep them short, honest and unscripted: the best ones sound like a friend's recommendation. Shot well, they're some of the most convincing footage you'll have.

## Conclusion

A highlight reel, a live stream and a few honest testimonials can make your Phoenix event last long after the last guest goes home. They help people remember it, and they help you fill the room next time.
