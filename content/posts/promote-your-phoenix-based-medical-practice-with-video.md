## Introduction

Phoenix has a busy, growing healthcare community, which means patients have plenty of choices. If you run a medical practice here, you need a way to stand out and earn trust before anyone walks through your door. Video is one of the best ways to do it. Here's what it can do for your practice:

1. **Build trust**: Video lets patients see your expertise, your credentials and the care you give. Stories from real patients add credibility no brochure can match.
2. **Explain your services**: A short video helps people understand what you treat and how you can help, in plain language.
3. **Set you apart**: Show what makes your approach to care different from every other practice in the Phoenix area.
4. **Help people find you**: Video on your website and social channels gives patients another way to discover your practice online.
5. **Stick in people's minds**: People remember what they watch better than what they skim. Helpful videos keep you connected with the patients you already have.

Here are three ways to put that to work.

## Highlighting Your Expertise

Short, clear videos about common conditions, treatments and procedures show patients you know your field. They answer questions before they're asked and make it easy for someone to choose you.

## Providing Virtual Tours

Lots of people feel nervous about visiting a new practice. A video tour of your office and a friendly hello from your staff helps them know what to expect. By the time they arrive, you're not strangers anymore.

## Sharing Patient Stories

Nothing is more convincing than a patient telling their own story. Filmed with care and their permission, these stories show the quality of care you give, attract new patients and remind current ones why they chose you.

## Conclusion

Show your expertise, open your doors with a virtual tour and let your patients speak for you. Do that, and your Phoenix practice will feel trusted and familiar long before the first appointment.
