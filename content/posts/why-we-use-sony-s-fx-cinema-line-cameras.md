## Introduction

Choosing the right camera matters. For a lot of our work, we reach for Sony's FX cinema line. Here's why.

## Image Quality

The FX cameras give us beautiful images with wide dynamic range, so we can hold detail in bright skies and deep shadows in the same shot. They also handle low light really well, which matters when we're filming a dim venue, a sunset or an interview by window light.

## Versatility

We film everything from documentaries to commercials to live events, so our cameras have to keep up. The FX line is compact and light enough to take almost anywhere, with high frame rates for slow motion, reliable autofocus and plenty of options for resolution and recording formats. The same camera can go from a tripod in a studio to a gimbal in the desert.

## Built to Work

These cameras are made for real productions. They're dependable on long shoot days, and they connect easily with the monitors, recorders, lenses and audio gear we rely on. That lets us build the right setup for each job.

## Easy to Use

When something great is happening in front of the lens, nobody wants to dig through menus. The FX line has clear controls, customizable buttons and helpful tools like focus peaking and zebras, so we can adjust on the fly and stay focused on the story.

## Conclusion

A camera doesn't make the film. People do. But good tools let us spend less time fighting the gear and more time getting the shot. Whether it's a commercial, a brand film or a documentary, the Sony FX line helps us bring your story to the screen looking its best.
