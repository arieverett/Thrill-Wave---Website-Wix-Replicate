## The Phoenix Metro Area

The Valley is a patchwork of cities, each with its own business community and its own mix of industries: healthcare, technology, finance, hospitality, aerospace, construction and more. As those businesses grow, video has become one of the best ways for them to stand out.

## The Cities We Work In

We film all over the Valley. A few of the places we spend our days:

1. **Phoenix**: The state capital and the heart of the metro, home to major companies and a big tourism industry.
2. **Mesa**: Arizona's third-largest city, with a strong aerospace and defense presence.
3. **Chandler**: A fast-growing city with a busy tech scene and a lively downtown.
4. **Glendale**: A diverse economy with manufacturing, retail and healthcare.
5. **Scottsdale**: A favorite with visitors, known for hospitality and home to a growing number of tech companies.
6. **Gilbert**: A strong local economy and a family-friendly reputation.
7. **Tempe**: Home to Arizona State University, a growing tech industry and a busy downtown full of restaurants and venues.
8. **Peoria**: A mix of neighborhoods, businesses and healthcare.
9. **Surprise**: Growing fast, with a big retail scene and plenty of families.
10. **Avondale**: A West Valley city with a growing healthcare scene.
11. **Goodyear**: A mix of manufacturing, retail and healthcare.
12. **Buckeye**: One of the fastest-growing cities in the Valley.
13. **El Mirage**: A small West Valley city with homes and local businesses side by side.
14. **Queen Creek**: A growing town at the southeast edge of the Valley.

Every one of these cities has its own character, and every business in them has a story. We'd love to help tell yours.

## What We Make

Every project starts with a SITREP, our research into your audience and your landscape, so we know which story is worth telling before we pick up a camera. Then we make it:

1. **Brand films**: The story of who you are and why it matters, told so people remember it.
2. **Commercials**: Short, sharp spots that make people stop and pay attention.
3. **Documentary-style testimonials**: Real people telling real stories, in their own words.
4. **Event coverage**: Conferences, festivals and launches, plus the aftermovie everyone shares.
5. **Product videos**: Your product, shot so people want it.
6. **Social video**: Short cuts sized for every platform, so one shoot keeps working for months.
7. **Commercial photography**: Stills with the same eye as our films, from scouting and art direction to the final edit, shot alongside the video or on their own.
8. **Audio and sound design**: Voiceover, score, sound design and mixing, so it sounds as good as it looks.
