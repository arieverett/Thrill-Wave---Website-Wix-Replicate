Video can help a small business get noticed, earn trust and win customers. But if you've never hired a production crew before, finding the right one can feel like a lot. Here's what we'd look for if we were in your shoes and hiring a crew in Phoenix.

### Experience and Portfolio

Start with the work. Look for a crew that has made films for businesses like yours, and watch their portfolio. Does their style fit what you have in mind? Do the videos make you feel something? If not, keep looking.

### Expertise

A good video takes a lot of different skills: directing, cinematography, lighting, sound, editing, color. Make sure the crew you hire can cover all of it, or knows exactly who to bring in. Gaps in any one of those show up on screen.

### Communication

You'll be working closely with this crew, so make sure they listen. Do they answer your questions quickly? Do they ask about your goals and your audience before they talk about cameras? A crew that does its homework up front makes a better film. (Ours starts with a SITREP, our research into your audience and landscape, before anyone picks up a camera.)

### Equipment

Cameras, lighting and sound gear all matter. Ask what they shoot on. You want professional kit and, more importantly, people who know how to use it well. Bad audio will sink a beautiful shot every time.

### Cost

Prices vary a lot depending on the scope, the gear and the crew's experience. Know your budget and look for a crew that can work with it honestly. Be careful with quotes that seem too good to be true; they usually are.

### Timelines

Good video takes time. Ask for a clear timeline up front, and look for a crew that tells you right away if anything changes.

### Reputation

Finally, check reviews and ask around. A crew with happy past clients is a crew that's likely to make you one, too.

Take your time with the choice. The right crew will make the whole process feel easy, and the film will show it.
