What sets us apart

SITREP: the step everyone else skips.

Step 03 is where we part ways with everyone else. SITREP is two things working together: software we design and build in-house, and a process sharpened over years of watching good stories get lost to guesswork. We saw the gaps, so we built something better.

Before a camera rolls, SITREP maps what your audience believes, where trust breaks down and which story will actually move people.

  1. 01

    Ingest

    Full-spectrum intelligence

    Internal data, audience intelligence, public sentiment, market signals, competitive positioning. Then wider: geopolitical factors, industry shifts, emerging narratives.

  2. 02

    Synthesize

    Data-driven sense-making

    Analysis across the full data set. Blind spots in comms, trust gaps with audience, narrative opportunities competitors missed.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Documentary-grade production

    Real environments, real people, observational production. Distribution plan built alongside production.
