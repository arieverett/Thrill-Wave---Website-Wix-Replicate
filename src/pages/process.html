SITREP

We do our homework first.

SITREP is our research. Before we film anything, we dig into your craft, your peers, your industry and the wider field you work in, and we come back with a point of view on the story worth telling. Timeline

As fast as a day. As long as a year.

When you're in a hurry, we can shoot and edit a film in a single day. Projects with more research, writing, shooting and editing can run as long as a year. Before we start, you'll get the plan, the schedule and the budget in writing, so there are no surprises halfway through. Here's the range we work in:

  • 1 dayOur fastest turnarounds, shoot to delivery
  • 1 yearOur longest projects, research to final edit
  • 1 crewFrom the first call to the final cut
