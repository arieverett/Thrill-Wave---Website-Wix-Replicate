About Thrill Wave · Est. 2018

Made of people, for people.

Thrill Wave is a video production company in Phoenix, Arizona. We bring the hearts of artists, the work ethic of military members and the curiosity of engineers and scientists to one job: working alongside people to help them tell their story clearly, honor what came before and move forward together.

Origin

Founded in 2018 in Phoenix, Arizona.

We started Thrill Wave because we love making things with people, and we kept watching the people get lost. Dated processes, smoke-and-mirrors pricing and endless bidding took the heart out of something that should be simple: good people, a true story and the care to tell it well. So we built the company we would want to hire, and we've tried to do right by everyone who walks through the door ever since. Here's where that has taken us so far:

Our values

What we care about.

We don't have a wall of slogans. We have a few things we care about, and they decide what we shoot, what we cut and when we push back. This is what you can hold us to:

We keep it true. Everyone on screen is a real person with something real at stake. We don't stage what should be felt, and we don't promise what we can't film.

We keep it simple. Untangling a complicated subject is our job, not the viewer's, so the message reaches people clearly.

We care about the craft. We love the problem-solving on set, the light and the edit that makes it all click. If a cut doesn't move us, we don't expect it to move anyone else, so we keep working until it does.

We stay accountable. You'll know who's on your project before the first shoot day, and one of our founders answers for how it turns out.

We talk straight. We're honest about scope, cost and timeline from the start, so there are no surprises, upsells or overpromises later.

We care what happens next. The final cut isn't the finish line. We pay attention to where the film lands and what it does for the people who watch it.

What guides us

Our mission, vision and charter.

Every company needs a compass, and this is ours. It says why we exist, where we hope it leads and the commitments we make to every client, crew member and person in front of our cameras. We'll start with the why:

Our mission

We help good people tell true stories, beautifully, so they reach the people who need to hear them.

Our vision

We want a world where the stories that matter are told as well as the ones that sell.

Our charter

These are the promises we make to everyone a project touches, and we keep them on every shoot:

  1. We tell the truth. What's on screen really happened, with people who chose to be there.
  2. We protect the people we film. We ask for consent first and treat everyone with dignity, especially when the story is personal.
  3. We research before we roll. Every project starts with a SITREP, our deep research into your world, so the story is earned and not guessed.
  4. We keep your trust. Your data, drafts and story stay confidential until you're ready to share them.
  5. We treat our crew right. Everyone who works with us is paid fairly and on time, and gets credit for the work they do.
  6. We leave it better. We leave every location and every community we film in better than we found it.
