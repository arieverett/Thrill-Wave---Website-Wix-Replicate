About Thrill Wave · Est. 2018

Made of people, for people.

Thrill Wave is a company of creatives with the hearts of artists, the work ethic of military members and the curiosity of engineers and scientists. We meet at that intersection for one reason: to work alongside other people, help them communicate better, honor what came before and move forward together.

Origin

Founded in 2018 in Phoenix, Arizona.

We started Thrill Wave because we love making things with people, and we kept watching the people get lost. Dated processes, smoke-and-mirrors pricing and endless bidding took the heart out of something that should be simple: good people, a true story and the care to tell it well. So we built the company we would want to hire, and we have tried to do right by everyone who walks through the door ever since.

Who we are

Three friends. One crew.

Before we were partners, we were friends. We are artists, operators and problem solvers who believe stories are how people come to understand each other. Everything we make is meant to move people, bring them closer and leave the folks on the other end a little better off for having watched.
