About Thrill Wave · Est. 2018

Made of people, for people.

Thrill Wave is a company of creatives with the hearts of artists, the work ethic of military members and the curiosity of engineers and scientists. We meet at that intersection for one reason: to work alongside other people, help them communicate better, honor what came before and move forward together.

Origin

Founded in 2018 in Phoenix, Arizona.

We started Thrill Wave because we love making things with people, and we kept watching the people get lost. Dated processes, smoke-and-mirrors pricing and endless bidding took the heart out of something that should be simple: good people, a true story and the care to tell it well. So we built the company we would want to hire, and we have tried to do right by everyone who walks through the door ever since.

How we work

Four promises we keep.

Every project runs on the same four promises. They decide what we shoot, what we cut and when we push back, and they're how you can hold us to our word.

  1. 01

    Make it true

    Real people, real stakes. No smoke and mirrors.

  2. 02

    Make it simple

    The hard work is ours. The message stays clear.

  3. 03

    Make it felt

    If it doesn't move us, it won't move anyone.

  4. 04

    Make it matter

    Leave people better off than we found them.

What guides us

Mission, values and charter.

A small crew still needs a compass. This is ours: why we exist, what we believe and the commitments we make to every client, crew member and person in front of our cameras.

Our mission

Help good people tell true stories, beautifully, so they move the people who need to hear them.

Our vision

A world where the stories that matter are told as well as the ones that sell.

Our values

  1. 01

    Craft first

    We love the work: the problem-solving on set, the light, the edit that makes it click. That care shows on screen.

  2. 02

    Truth on camera

    Real people and real stakes. We don't stage what should be felt or promise what we can't film.

  3. 03

    Founders accountable

    You know who's on your project before day one, and one of us answers for how it turns out.

  4. 04

    Honest collaboration

    Straight talk about scope, cost and timeline. No surprises, no upsells, no overpromising.

  5. 05

    Built to perform

    The final cut isn't the finish line. We care where the film lands and what it does once it's live.

Our charter

  1. We tell the truth. What's on screen really happened, with people who chose to be there.
  2. We protect the people we film. Consent first and dignity always, especially when the story is personal.
  3. We research before we roll. Every project starts with a SITREP, so the story is earned, not guessed.
  4. We keep your trust. Your data, drafts and story stay confidential until you're ready to share them.
  5. We treat our crew right. Fair pay, on time, with credit for the work they do.
  6. We leave it better. Every location and every community we film in, better than we found it.
