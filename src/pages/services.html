How we work

Research first, then the camera.

A lot of people find us after a video that looked great and changed nothing. That usually happens when nobody asked what the film was for. So we start there: we research your field, your peers and the people you need to reach, and then we bring you a point of view on the story worth telling. You came to us for our expertise, and we think the honest thing is to use it.

A founder leads every project from the first call to the final cut, and the same crew stays with it the whole way. We plan the short cuts, stills and sound before we roll, so one shoot keeps working for months instead of disappearing after a single post. We show up early, run calm sets, treat your people and your spaces with respect, and deliver when we said we would.
