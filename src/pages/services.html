What we hear

The things people tell us on the first call.

A lot of people find us after a video let them down somewhere else. The same few stories come up again and again, so here they are, along with what we do about each one:

  1. “Our last video looked great and did nothing.”

    That usually happens when nobody asked what the film was for. We start there, with research into your field and the people you need to reach, and build everything around the answer.

    What helps: Strategy & creative, SITREP research

  2. “The story got lost between vendors.”

    A strategist here, a crew there and an editor somewhere else is a lot of handoffs. The same crew carries your film from the first call to the final cut, so nothing gets dropped along the way.

    What helps: Pre-production, production, post-production

  3. “We paid for people we never met.”

    Big shops bill for layers you never see. A founder leads every project with us, we move quickly, and the budget ends up on screen.

    What helps: Straight pricing, a founder on every project

  4. “It was gone after one post.”

    One shoot should keep working for months. We plan the short cuts and stills before we roll, so nothing goes to waste.

    What helps: Post-production, photography
